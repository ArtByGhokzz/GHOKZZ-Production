# Teaser plan: "20th November draft" (Gustav B Achu, seven Destiny Thieves video)

Method: Gustav Storytelling & Retention Editing Framework (Tasks 1, 2, 3, 8, 10, 12, 15 applied to a teaser).
Source: transcript only (`attachments/20th_November_draft_transcript.txt`). No footage was reviewed.
Timestamps are caption-level (whole seconds, line start). Fine-tune cut points on the actual footage.
Limit: 50 seconds.

## Story read (Task 1)
- Core message: most people lack implementation, not information. Seven habits steal your destiny.
- Viewer promise: the seven Destiny Thieves.
- Emotional journey: curiosity, vulnerability, recognition, resolve.
- Strongest statement: "not information deficit, it is implementation deficit" (0:57-1:07).
- Most emotional story: Gustav's fear of the camera (4:58-5:41).
- Weakest section for a teaser: the long explanatory passages (10,000 hours, closet and materialism).

## Editing direction for Option A (Tasks 8, 10, 12)
- Open cold on a tight punch-in on Gustav, no music, only room tone, with the first line captioned.
- 4:58 to 5:21: keep it intimate and slow, with a soft low pad under it.
- Each "what if" gets its own on-screen question, using his exact words: "WHAT IF MY ACCENT IS BAD?", "WHAT IF I DON'T LOOK THE PART?", "WHAT IF MY GRAMMAR ISN'T PERFECT?". Add a soft tick on each one.
- B-roll: real early footage of Gustav on camera, if it exists (before and after). Otherwise stay on him. No generic stock.
- "...deceiving you" (5:39-5:41): half-second of silence, then a punch-in and emphasize "DECEIVING YOU".
- 0:57 thesis: full-screen text. "INFORMATION DEFICIT" struck through, "IMPLEMENTATION DEFICIT" highlighted. Music rises and pace picks up.
- "Seven Thieves": on-screen text "7 DESTINY THIEVES" with a sound hit, then hard-stop the music and cut to the title card or intro.
- No CTA in the teaser. The CTA belongs inside the video.
